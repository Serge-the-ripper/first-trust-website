Customer Service & Policies

Delivery

We offer same-day delivery across Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx, and Staten Island on orders placed before 2pm. Standard delivery windows are scheduled at checkout, and our team will call ahead 30 minutes before arrival.

Service Request Form

Need a repair or maintenance visit? Email support@firsttrustappliances.com with your order number and a description of the issue, and our service team will follow up within one business day to schedule a visit.

Authorized Servicers

All warranty repairs are performed by First Trust's in-house NYC technicians or manufacturer-authorized partners. Using a non-authorized servicer may void your appliance's warranty coverage.

Payment

We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and Apple Pay. NYC sales tax (8.875%) is calculated automatically at checkout based on your delivery address.

Warranty

Every appliance includes full manufacturer warranty coverage from the date of delivery. Warranty length varies by product category and is listed on each product's detail view.

Accessibility Policy

First Trust is committed to an accessible shopping experience for all customers. If you encounter any barrier using this site, contact us at support@firsttrustappliances.com and we'll work with you directly.

Financing

Flexible monthly financing is available on major appliance purchases at checkout, subject to approval. Rates and terms are shown before you confirm your order — no impact to your credit to check eligibility.

Returns & Exchanges

Unopened, uninstalled items may be returned within 30 days for a full refund. Installed major appliances are covered by manufacturer warranty rather than the standard return window — contact us and we'll walk you through the right option.

Terms & Policies

By ordering from First Trust Home Care Appliances, you agree to our standard terms of sale, delivery, and warranty policies outlined throughout this page. Pricing and availability are subject to change without notice.

Installation & Removal

Professional installation and haul-away of your old unit can be added to any major appliance order at checkout. Our delivery team handles hookup for standard installations on the day of delivery.

Contact Us

Call us at +1 (858) 308-1857 or email support@firsttrustappliances.com. Our showroom at 450 5th Avenue is open Mon–Sat 8am–10pm and Sun 10am–8pm.

Parts & Accessories

Looking for a replacement filter, hose, or part for an appliance you bought from us? Reach out with your order number and model, and our team will source the correct part for you.
